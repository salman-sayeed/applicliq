Digital Marketing Manager
Fernway Outdoor Co. · Denver, CO

Fernway makes durable gear for people who spend their weekends outside. We're hiring a Digital Marketing Manager to grow our direct-to-consumer business.

In this role you will:
- Plan and run paid social campaigns on Meta Ads and TikTok Ads
- Own SEO and content marketing for our blog and product pages
- Manage email marketing and lifecycle flows in Klaviyo
- Report on campaign performance with Google Analytics (GA4) and Looker Studio
- Run A/B testing on landing pages to improve conversion rate optimization
- Work with influencers on influencer marketing programs
- Manage a monthly paid media budget and report on return on ad spend

You have:
- 4+ years in digital marketing, ideally at an e-commerce brand
- Hands-on experience with Google Ads and Meta Ads
- Strong copywriting and storytelling skills
- Experience with Shopify
- Excellent communication and project management skills

Nice to have:
- Experience with Canva or Adobe Photoshop
- Familiarity with HubSpot
- A love of the outdoors

Our customer journey starts outside: we want every customer journey to end with a product that lasts. Paid social and customer journey work go hand in hand.
