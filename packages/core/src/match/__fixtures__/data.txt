Data Scientist, Growth

About us

- Create dashboards in Tableau for product and marketing stakeholders
- Communicate results clearly to non-technical partners

Minimum qualifications
- MS or PhD in Statistics, Computer Science or a related quantitative field
- 3+ years of experience in data science or analytics
- Strong skills in Python (Pandas, NumPy) and SQL
- Solid grounding in statistics, hypothesis testing and experiment design
- Experience with machine learning and regression analysis

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with causal inference
- Familiarity with R
- Experience with Spark or Databricks
- Healthcare experience
